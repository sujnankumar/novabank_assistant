## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the eligibility criteria, loan terms, interest rates, repayment policies, and documentation requirements for NovaBank education loans. These loans are designed to help students finance their higher education at recognized institutions both within India and abroad.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **Student Applicant:** The student who is the primary beneficiary of the education loan.
- **Co-applicant:** A parent, guardian, or spouse who applies jointly with the student and shares the repayment liability.
- **Moratorium Period:** The period during which the borrower is not required to make any EMI payments. This typically covers the course duration plus a grace period after course completion.
- **Collateral:** Security pledged against the loan, such as property, fixed deposits, or other acceptable assets.
- **Disbursement:** The release of the loan amount, typically made directly to the educational institution.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### 4.1 Student Applicant

- Must be an Indian citizen.
- Must be between 18 and 35 years of age at the time of application.
- Must have secured admission to a recognized educational institution through a valid admission letter.
- Must have a satisfactory academic record (minimum 50% aggregate in the qualifying examination, or equivalent grade).

### 4.2 Co-applicant (Mandatory)

- A co-applicant is mandatory for all education loans. The co-applicant may be a parent, guardian, or spouse.
- **Domestic Loan:** Co-applicant minimum monthly income of INR 15,000 and minimum credit score of 600.
- **International Loan:** Co-applicant minimum monthly income of INR 25,000 and minimum credit score of 650.
- The co-applicant is jointly liable for the repayment of the loan.

### 4.3 Eligible Education Programs

Education loans are available for the following types of programs:

**Domestic Programs:**
- Undergraduate degrees (B.Tech, B.E., B.Sc., B.Com, BA, BBA, etc.)
- Postgraduate degrees (M.Tech, M.E., M.Sc., M.Com, MA, MBA, MCA, etc.)
- Professional courses (Medicine, Law, Architecture, Chartered Accountancy, etc.)
- Diploma and certificate programs from recognized institutions
- Doctoral and research programs (Ph.D., M.Phil.)

**International Programs:**
- Undergraduate and postgraduate programs at recognized foreign universities
- MBA programs at accredited business schools
- Professional certification programs (CFA, CPA, etc.)
- Research programs at internationally recognized institutions

### 4.4 Eligible Institutions

- **Domestic:** Institutions recognized by UGC, AICTE, MCI, BCI, or respective regulatory bodies. Autonomous institutions of national importance (IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMS, etc.) are eligible.
- **International:** Universities ranked within the top 500 of QS World Rankings, Times Higher Education Rankings, or equivalent global rankings. NovaBank maintains a list of pre-approved international institutions.

## 9. Application Process

1. **Admission Confirmation:** The student must first secure admission to an eligible institution.
2. **Application:** The student and co-applicant submit the loan application with all required documents at a NovaBank branch or through the online portal.
3. **Document Verification:** NovaBank verifies all submitted documents including admission letter, fee structure, and co-applicant's financial documents.
4. **Credit Assessment:** NovaBank evaluates the co-applicant's creditworthiness and income adequacy.
5. **Property Valuation (if collateral is required):** An independent valuation is conducted for collateral offered.
6. **Sanction:** Upon approval, a sanction letter is issued detailing the loan amount, interest rate, tenure, and terms.
7. **Agreement Execution:** The student and co-applicant sign the loan agreement.
8. **Disbursement:** The loan is disbursed as per the disbursement schedule.

Standard processing time: 7 to 15 working days.

## 10. Disbursement

- Education loan amounts are disbursed directly to the educational institution's designated bank account.
- Disbursement is made in installments aligned with the academic semester or year fee schedule.
- Living expenses and other non-tuition components may be disbursed to the student's or co-applicant's NovaBank account.
- For international loans, disbursement may be made in foreign currency to the institution's account via wire transfer.

## 11. Moratorium Period

- The moratorium period includes the course duration plus a grace period of 6 months after course completion or 12 months after securing employment, whichever is earlier.
- During the moratorium period, no EMI payments are required.
- However, interest continues to accrue during the moratorium period. Students may opt to pay the interest during this period (called a "partial interest servicing" option) to reduce the overall cost.
- If interest is not paid during the moratorium, it is capitalized (added to the principal) at the start of the repayment period.

---

## 12. Repayment

- Repayment begins after the moratorium period ends.
- **Domestic Loan:** Maximum repayment tenure of 10 years.
- **International Loan:** Maximum repayment tenure of 15 years.
- EMIs are calculated on the outstanding amount (principal plus any capitalized interest) using the reducing balance method.
- EMIs can be paid via auto-debit from a NovaBank account or NACH mandate.

### 12.1 Repayment Example

For a Domestic Education Loan of INR 10,00,000 at 9.50% p.a. for 10 years (after moratorium):

- Approximate EMI: INR 12,940
- Total Interest Payable: INR 5,52,800
- Total Amount Payable: INR 15,52,800

*Note: If interest during the moratorium period is not serviced, the capitalized amount will increase the EMI.*

## 13. Prepayment and Foreclosure

### 13.1 Prepayment

- Partial prepayment is allowed at any time after the moratorium period begins, with no prepayment charges.
- Minimum prepayment amount: INR 25,000 or 5% of outstanding principal, whichever is higher.

### 13.2 Foreclosure

- Full foreclosure is allowed at any time with no foreclosure charges.
- NovaBank does not levy any penalty for early repayment of education loans.
- A written request must be submitted at least 7 working days before the intended foreclosure date.

---

## 14. Late Payment and Default

- A late payment fee of 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount is charged for payments received after the due date.
- If an EMI remains unpaid for more than 90 days after the due date, the account is classified as NPA.
- Default may result in:
  - Recovery proceedings against the co-applicant
  - Liquidation of collateral (if applicable)
  - Legal action
  - Negative credit reporting to credit bureaus for both the student and co-applicant

---

## 15. Course Change or Discontinuation

- If the student changes the course or institution, the revised fee structure must be submitted to NovaBank. The loan may be restructured accordingly.
- If the student discontinues the course:
  - Repayment becomes immediately due after a 3-month grace period.
  - The moratorium benefit is withdrawn.
  - Interest continues to accrue on the outstanding amount.

## 16. Exceptions

- NovaBank may waive collateral requirements on a case-by-case basis for meritorious students.
