## 2. Definitions

- **EMI (Equated Monthly Installment):** A fixed monthly payment made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month, covering both principal and interest.
- **Tenure:** The total duration of the loan in years or months.
- **Processing Fee:** A one-time fee charged by the bank for processing the loan application.
- **Foreclosure:** Full repayment of the outstanding loan amount before the scheduled tenure ends.
- **Prepayment:** Partial repayment of the outstanding principal before the scheduled EMI date.
- **Co-applicant:** An additional person who applies for the loan jointly with the primary applicant. The co-applicant shares the liability for loan repayment.
- **Disbursement:** The release of the sanctioned loan amount to the borrower or directly to the property seller/builder.
- **Collateral:** The property being purchased or renovated, which is pledged as security for the loan.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### 4.1 Age Requirements

- **Home Loan Standard:** Applicant must be between 21 and 60 years of age at the time of application. The loan tenure must not extend beyond the applicant's 65th birthday.
- **Home Loan Premium:** Applicant must be between 25 and 58 years of age at the time of application. The loan tenure must not extend beyond the applicant's 62nd birthday.
- **Home Improvement Loan:** Applicant must be between 23 and 60 years of age at the time of application.

### 4.2 Income Requirements

- **Home Loan Standard:** Minimum monthly income of INR 30,000.
- **Home Loan Premium:** Minimum monthly income of INR 75,000.
- **Home Improvement Loan:** Minimum monthly income of INR 25,000.

Income is assessed based on the average of the last 3 to 6 months of salary slips, or the last 2 to 3 years of IT Returns for self-employed applicants.

### 4.3 Credit Score Requirements

- **Home Loan Standard:** Minimum credit score of 700.
- **Home Loan Premium:** Minimum credit score of 750.
- **Home Improvement Loan:** Minimum credit score of 680.

A higher credit score may result in preferential interest rates. NovaBank reserves the right to reject applications with adverse credit histories even if the minimum score is met.

### 4.4 Employment

- Salaried employees must have a minimum of 2 years of total work experience and at least 6 months with the current employer.
- Self-employed individuals must demonstrate a minimum of 3 years of business continuity.
- Professionals such as doctors, chartered accountants, and architects are eligible under special professional schemes.

### 4.5 Nationality and Residency

- The applicant must be an Indian resident or a Non-Resident Indian (NRI) with valid documentation.
- NRI applications may be subject to additional scrutiny and documentation requirements.

## 9. Application Process

1. **Inquiry:** The customer can inquire about home loan products through NovaBank branches, the website, or the customer service helpline.
2. **Application Submission:** The customer submits the completed application form along with all required documents.
3. **Document Verification:** NovaBank verifies all submitted documents, including identity, income, and property documents.
4. **Credit Assessment:** NovaBank assesses the applicant's creditworthiness based on credit score, income, existing obligations, and employment stability.
5. **Property Valuation:** An independent valuation of the property is conducted by a NovaBank-empaneled valuer.
6. **Legal Verification:** NovaBank's legal team verifies the title and ownership of the property.
7. **Loan Sanction:** Upon satisfactory completion of all checks, NovaBank issues a loan sanction letter detailing the approved amount, interest rate, tenure, and terms.
8. **Acceptance:** The customer reviews and accepts the sanction letter, including all terms and conditions.
9. **Disbursement:** The loan amount is disbursed as per the disbursement schedule, typically linked to construction milestones for under-construction properties.

The standard processing time is 7 to 14 working days from the date of complete application submission.

## 10. Disbursement

- For ready-to-move-in properties, disbursement is made in a single tranche directly to the seller's account.
- For under-construction properties, disbursement is made in stages linked to construction progress, as certified by the project engineer.
- The first EMI becomes due 30 days after the first disbursement.
- During the construction period, only the interest component (pre-EMI interest) is payable on the disbursed amount.

## 11. EMI and Repayment

- EMIs are calculated using the reducing balance method.
- EMIs are due on the same date each month as specified in the loan agreement.
- EMIs can be paid via auto-debit from a NovaBank savings or salary account, NACH mandate, or post-dated cheques.
- An EMI calculator is available on the NovaBank website and mobile app to estimate monthly payments.

## 12. Prepayment and Foreclosure

### 12.1 Prepayment

- Partial prepayment of the outstanding principal is allowed after a minimum of 6 EMI payments.
- No prepayment charges are levied for floating-rate home loans.
- Prepayment must be in multiples of INR 10,000.
- A maximum of 4 prepayments are allowed per financial year.

### 12.2 Foreclosure

- Full foreclosure (complete repayment of the outstanding loan) is permitted after a minimum of 12 EMI payments.
- No foreclosure charges are levied for floating-rate home loans.
- For fixed-rate loans, a foreclosure charge of 2% of the outstanding principal may apply.
- The customer must submit a written foreclosure request at least 15 working days before the intended date.

---

## 13. Late Payment and Default

- A late payment fee of 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount is charged for payments received after the due date.
- If an EMI is not paid within 90 days of the due date, the loan account is classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA).
- Repeated defaults may result in legal action and recovery proceedings.
- NovaBank will report all overdue accounts to credit bureaus, which may adversely affect the borrower's credit score.
- The borrower will receive reminders via SMS, email, and phone calls before any recovery action is initiated.

---

## 14. Co-applicants

- A co-applicant is mandatory if the property is jointly owned.
- Eligible co-applicants include spouse, parents, or children of the primary borrower.
- The co-applicant's income is considered for determining the loan eligibility and amount.
- The co-applicant must provide separate identity, income, and KYC documents.
- Both the primary applicant and co-applicant are equally liable for loan repayment.

## 15. Property Requirements

- The property must be located within India.
- The property must have clear and marketable title.
- The property must not be subject to any litigation, encumbrance, or legal dispute.
- Construction must comply with local building regulations and approvals.
- For the Home Improvement Loan, the applicant must already own the property and provide proof of ownership.
