## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the features, eligibility criteria, interest rates, tenure options, maturity procedures, and premature withdrawal rules for NovaBank fixed deposit products. Fixed deposits (FDs) are time-bound savings instruments that offer a guaranteed return at a fixed interest rate.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **Fixed Deposit (FD):** A savings instrument where a lump-sum amount is deposited for a fixed period at a predetermined interest rate.
- **Maturity:** The date on which the fixed deposit tenure ends and the depositor is entitled to receive the principal plus accrued interest.
- **Premature Withdrawal:** Closing the FD before its maturity date.
- **Auto-Renewal:** Automatic renewal of the FD for the same tenure at the prevailing interest rate upon maturity, if no other instruction is given.
- **Cumulative FD:** Interest is compounded and paid at maturity along with the principal.
- **Non-Cumulative FD:** Interest is paid out periodically (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or annually) during the FD tenure.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### 4.1 Regular Fixed Deposit

- Indian resident aged 18 years or above.
- Valid KYC documents (PAN Card, Aadhaar Card).
- Must hold or open a NovaBank savings or current account for interest credit and maturity proceeds.

### 4.2 Tax Saver Fixed Deposit

- Indian resident aged 18 years or above.
- Valid KYC documents.
- Maximum deposit of INR 1,50,000 per financial year (eligible for deduction under Section 80C).
- Mandatory lock-in period of 5 years. No premature withdrawal allowed.

### 4.3 Senior Citizen Fixed Deposit

- Indian resident aged 60 years or above.
- Valid KYC documents and proof of age.
- Offers a higher interest rate as a benefit for senior citizens.
- Must hold or open a NovaBank savings account.

### 4.4 Flexi Fixed Deposit

- Indian resident aged 18 years or above.
- Valid KYC documents.
- Must have a linked NovaBank savings account.
- Minimum deposit of INR 25,000.
- The Flexi FD is linked to the savings account and provides a sweep-in/sweep-out facility.

## 10. Maturity

### 10.1 Maturity Proceeds

- On maturity, the principal and accrued interest (for cumulative FDs) are credited to the linked NovaBank savings account.
- For non-cumulative FDs, only the principal is credited at maturity (interest has already been paid periodically).
- NovaBank sends a maturity reminder via SMS and email 7 days before the maturity date.

### 10.2 Auto-Renewal

- If the customer does not provide maturity instructions, the FD is automatically renewed for the same tenure at the prevailing interest rate on the maturity date.
- Customers can opt out of auto-renewal at the time of FD booking or through internet banking.
- After auto-renewal, the customer has a 7-day grace period to withdraw or modify the renewed FD without penalty.

## 11. Premature Withdrawal

### 11.1 Regular FD and Senior Citizen FD

- Premature withdrawal is allowed at any time after 7 days from the date of deposit.
- A penalty of 1.00% is deducted from the applicable interest rate for the period the deposit was held.
- Example: If an FD booked for 3 years at 6.50% is prematurely withdrawn after 1 year, the applicable rate would be the 1-year rate (6.00%) minus the 1.00% penalty = 5.00% for the period held.

### 11.2 Tax Saver FD

- Premature withdrawal is **not allowed** during the 5-year lock-in period.
- The FD cannot be pledged, assigned, or used as collateral.

### 11.3 Flexi FD

- The Flexi FD operates with a sweep-in/sweep-out mechanism:
  - If the linked savings account balance falls below a specified threshold, funds are automatically swept out from the FD (in multiples of INR 5,000) to the savings account.
  - If the linked savings account balance exceeds a specified threshold, excess funds are automatically swept into the FD.
- The sweep-out is treated as partial premature withdrawal. Interest on the withdrawn portion is calculated at the applicable rate minus 0.50% penalty.
- The remaining FD balance continues to earn the original interest rate.

- Interest rate on loan against FD: FD interest rate + 2.00%.

- The FD is pledged to NovaBank until the loan is repaid.

## 14. Tax Implications

- Interest earned on fixed deposits is subject to taxation as per the account holder's income tax slab.
- Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) is applicable if the interest earned across all FDs exceeds INR 40,000 in a financial year (INR 50,000 for senior citizens).
- To avoid TDS (if total income is below the taxable threshold), the customer may submit Form 15G (or Form 15H for senior citizens) at the beginning of the financial year.
- The Tax Saver FD qualifies for deduction under Section 80C up to INR 1,50,000 per financial year.

*Note: This is general information. Customers should consult a tax professional for personalized tax advice.*

## 15. FD Closure

- The customer can close an FD at maturity or prematurely (subject to conditions in Section 11).
- Closure can be initiated at a branch or through internet banking.
- Maturity proceeds are credited to the linked savings account within 1 working day.
- For premature closure, interest is recalculated at the applicable rate minus the penalty.

### Q: What is the Senior Citizen FD interest rate?

### Q: How can I open an FD online?
**A:** Log in to NovaBank internet banking or the mobile app, navigate to Fixed Deposits, select "Book New FD," choose the type, amount, and tenure, and confirm. The amount is debited from your linked savings account.
