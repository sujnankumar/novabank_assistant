- **Secure Login:** Internet banking and mobile app use multi-factor authentication including password, OTP, and optional biometric verification.

## 3. Customer Security Responsibilities

### 3.1 Password and PIN Security

- **Never share** your internet banking password, mobile banking PIN, ATM PIN, or any OTP with anyone, including NovaBank employees. NovaBank will never ask for your password or PIN.
- Use strong passwords with a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters (minimum 8 characters).
- Change your internet banking password every 90 days.
- Do not use the same password for banking and other online services.
- Do not write down passwords or PINs in easily accessible locations.
- Do not use easily guessable PINs such as birth dates, phone numbers, or sequential numbers (e.g., 1234, 0000).

### 3.2 OTP Safety

- OTPs are sent exclusively to your registered mobile number or email address.
- **Never share** an OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be from NovaBank.
- OTPs are valid for a limited time (typically 5 minutes) and for a single transaction.
- If you receive an OTP for a transaction you did not initiate, do not enter it. Report it immediately.

### 3.3 Device Security

- Keep your mobile phone and computer operating systems updated with the latest security patches.
- Install and maintain reputable antivirus software on your devices.
- Do not install banking apps from unofficial sources. Only download the NovaBank mobile app from the official Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
- Enable screen lock (PIN, fingerprint, or face recognition) on your mobile phone.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi for banking transactions. Use a secure, private connection.

### 3.4 Online Safety

- Always access NovaBank internet banking by typing the URL directly in the browser address bar. Do not click on links in emails or messages.
- Verify the website URL starts with "https://" and displays a padlock icon.
- Log out of internet banking after every session. Do not rely on just closing the browser.
- Clear browser cache and cookies regularly, especially on shared or public computers.
- Do not save banking passwords in the browser's auto-fill feature.

## 4. Recognizing Common Fraud Types

### 4.1 Phishing

- **What it is:** Fraudulent emails, SMS messages, or websites that impersonate NovaBank to trick customers into revealing personal information, passwords, or OTPs.
- **How to identify:**
  - Emails from unfamiliar addresses or with misspelled NovaBank domains.
  - Messages creating urgency (e.g., "Your account will be blocked in 24 hours").
  - Links that lead to websites that look similar to but are not the official NovaBank website.
  - Requests for passwords, PINs, OTPs, or full card numbers via email or SMS.
- **What to do:** Do not click any links. Do not provide any information. Report the phishing attempt to NovaBank.

### 4.2 Vishing (Voice Phishing)

- **What it is:** Phone calls from fraudsters pretending to be NovaBank employees, requesting sensitive information.
- **How to identify:**
  - Callers asking for your full card number, CVV, OTP, or internet banking password.
  - Callers creating urgency about account suspension, KYC updates, or reward claims.
  - Callers asking you to install remote-access apps.
- **What to do:** Hang up immediately. NovaBank will never ask for your password, OTP, or CVV over the phone.

### 4.3 Smishing (SMS Phishing)

- **What it is:** Fraudulent SMS messages containing malicious links or requesting sensitive information.
- **How to identify:**
  - SMS from unknown numbers claiming to be NovaBank.
  - Messages with links to "verify your account" or "claim a reward."
  - Messages asking you to call a non-NovaBank number.
- **What to do:** Do not click links in suspicious SMS messages. Report the SMS to NovaBank.

### 4.4 Card Skimming

- **What it is:** Devices installed on ATMs or POS machines that capture card data.
- **How to identify:**
  - Check for loose or unusual attachments on the ATM card slot.
  - Cover the keypad while entering your PIN.
  - Prefer NovaBank ATMs or ATMs in well-lit, secure locations.
- **What to do:** If you suspect skimming, do not use the ATM. Report it to NovaBank immediately.

### 4.5 Social Engineering

- **What it is:** Manipulation techniques used to trick customers into performing actions or revealing information.
- **How to identify:**
  - Unsolicited calls or messages offering too-good-to-be-true deals.
  - Requests to transfer money for "verification" or "test transactions."
  - Pressure to act quickly without time to verify.
- **What to do:** Verify any request independently by calling the official NovaBank helpline (1800-123-4567).

### 4.6 SIM Swap Fraud

- **What it is:** Fraudsters obtain a duplicate SIM card for your registered mobile number, intercepting OTPs and banking alerts.
- **How to identify:**
  - Your mobile phone suddenly loses network signal for an extended period.
  - You stop receiving SMS messages and calls.
  - You receive unexpected notifications about SIM changes from your telecom provider.
- **What to do:** Contact your telecom provider immediately. Call NovaBank to block your accounts and cards.

### 5.2 What to Report

When reporting suspicious activity, provide:

- Your account number or customer ID
- Date and time of the suspicious activity
- Transaction ID (if a transaction was involved)
- Description of what happened
- Any suspicious communication received (emails, SMS, calls)
- Actions already taken (e.g., changed password, blocked card)

### 5.3 Reporting Timeline

- Report unauthorized transactions as soon as they are detected.
- Reporting within 24 hours: Maximum protection under NovaBank's zero-liability policy.
- Reporting within 3 days: Limited liability (maximum INR 5,000).
- Reporting after 3 days: Customer may be liable for the full amount of unauthorized transactions.

## 6. Account and Card Blocking

### 6.1 Emergency Card Blocking

If a card is lost, stolen, or compromised:

1. **Immediately block** the card through:
   - NovaBank Mobile App (Cards > Block Card)
   - Internet Banking (Cards > Block/Deactivate)
   - Helpline: 1800-123-4567
2. A blocked card cannot be unblocked. A replacement card will be issued within 5-7 working days.

### 6.2 Account Blocking

If the account is compromised:

1. Contact the helpline or visit a branch immediately.
2. Request a temporary account freeze.
3. NovaBank will block all debit channels while keeping credit channels open (to receive salary, refunds, etc.).
4. After verification and investigation, the account is unfrozen and new credentials are issued.

### 6.3 Internet Banking and Mobile Banking Lock

- If unauthorized access is suspected, immediately change the internet banking password.
- Lock mobile banking through the app (Settings > Lock Account) or by calling the helpline.
- A temporary lock can be self-released after 24 hours through the registered mobile number.

## 7. Fraud Investigation Process

1. **Acknowledgment:** NovaBank acknowledges the fraud report within 24 hours.
2. **Investigation:** An internal investigation team reviews transaction logs, device information, and communication records.
3. **Provisional Credit:** For zero-liability-eligible cases, a provisional credit may be issued within 10 working days.
4. **Resolution:** The investigation is completed within:
   - 10 working days for domestic transactions.
   - 30 to 45 working days for international transactions.
5. **Outcome Notification:** The customer is informed of the investigation outcome via email and SMS.
6. **Final Settlement:** If the claim is valid, the final credit is processed. If not valid, the provisional credit (if issued) is reversed with prior notice.
