## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the procedures and rules for managing NovaBank accounts throughout their lifecycle -- from opening to closure. It covers profile updates, communication preferences, nominee management, dormancy, and account closure.

## 2. Account Opening

### 2.1 Types of Accounts

NovaBank offers the following account types:

- **Savings Account:** For individuals (Basic, Premium, Zero Balance -- see Savings Account Policy for details).
- **Current Account:** For businesses and professionals (Business Current, Premium Current).
- **Salary Account:** Opened in partnership with employers for salary disbursement. Operates like a Zero Balance Savings Account with additional benefits.

### 2.2 Required Documents for Account Opening

| Document | Savings Account | Current Account |
|---|---|---|
| PAN Card | Mandatory | Mandatory |
| Aadhaar Card | Mandatory | Mandatory |
| Proof of Address | Mandatory | Mandatory |
| Passport-size Photographs | 2 | 2 |
| Income Proof (for Premium) | Yes | Not required |
| Business Registration | No | Mandatory |
| GST Certificate | No | Mandatory |
| Board Resolution (Companies) | No | Required for companies |

### 2.3 Account Opening Process

1. **Application:** Submit the application form at a NovaBank branch or online.
2. **Document Submission:** Provide all required KYC and identity documents.
3. **Verification:** NovaBank verifies documents and conducts in-person verification (IPV) or video KYC for online applications.
4. **Initial Deposit:** Make the required initial deposit (varies by account type; not required for Zero Balance accounts).
5. **Account Activation:** Account is activated within 1 to 3 working days.
6. **Welcome Kit:** The customer receives a welcome kit containing the account number, debit card, cheque book, and internet banking credentials within 5 to 7 working days.

### 2.4 Minor Accounts

- Minors (below 18 years) can have savings accounts operated by a natural guardian (parent or court-appointed guardian).
- Upon turning 18, the account must be converted to an individual account with the minor's own KYC documents.

### 3.2 Name Change

- A name change requires a visit to a NovaBank branch.
- Supporting documents: Updated PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Gazette notification (if applicable), or marriage certificate.
- Name changes are processed within 5 to 7 working days after verification.

### 3.3 Address Update

- Can be updated online through internet banking or the mobile app by uploading a new proof of address.
- Acceptable documents: Updated Aadhaar, passport, utility bill (not older than 3 months), or rent agreement.
- Address updates are processed within 2 to 3 working days.

### 3.4 Phone Number Update

- For security reasons, phone number updates require a branch visit or ATM Banking channel.
- The customer must provide identity proof at the branch.
- The new phone number is activated within 24 hours.

### 3.5 Email Address Update

- Can be updated through internet banking, mobile app, or a branch visit.
- A verification email is sent to the new email address.
- The update takes effect immediately upon verification.

## 5. Nominee Management

### 5.1 What is a Nominee?

A nominee is a person designated by the account holder to receive the account balance in the event of the account holder's death. Nomination is a convenience facility to provide immediate access and does not override a legal will or succession laws.

### 5.2 Adding or Updating a Nominee

- Can be done at the time of account opening or any time after.
- **At Branch:** Submit a nomination form (Form DA1 for new nomination, Form DA2 for change/cancellation).
- **Online:** Through internet banking under Account Management > Nomination.
- Only one nominee per individual account.
- For joint accounts, nomination applies to the account, and the surviving holder's rights take precedence.

### 5.3 Nominee Details Required

- Full name of the nominee
- Relationship with the account holder
- Date of birth of the nominee
- Address of the nominee
- If the nominee is a minor, details of the guardian

## 6. Account Status

### 6.1 Active

- The account is fully operational with no restrictions.
- All transactions, services, and facilities are available.

### 6.2 Dormant / Inactive

- An account is classified as **inactive** if there are no customer-initiated transactions for 12 continuous months.
- An account is classified as **dormant** if there are no customer-initiated transactions for 24 continuous months.
- **Restrictions on dormant accounts:**
  - Debit transactions are blocked.
  - Credit transactions (salary, transfers received) continue to be accepted.
  - Internet banking and mobile banking access may be restricted.
- **Reactivation:**
  - Visit a NovaBank branch with valid KYC documents.
  - Submit a reactivation request form.
  - The account is reactivated within 1 to 2 working days.
  - A reactivation fee of INR 200 may apply.

### 6.3 Frozen

- An account may be frozen by NovaBank due to:
  - Court orders or regulatory directives
  - Suspicious activity detected by the fraud monitoring system
  - Incomplete KYC or expired KYC documents
- Frozen accounts cannot process any debit or credit transactions.
- The customer is notified of the freeze and the reason.
- To unfreeze: comply with the stated requirement (e.g., update KYC, respond to the regulatory inquiry).

### 6.4 Closed

- The account has been permanently closed.
- No further transactions are possible.
- Account closure is irreversible.

## 7. Multiple Accounts

- A customer may hold multiple accounts with NovaBank:
  - Multiple savings accounts of different types (e.g., one Basic and one Premium).
  - A savings account and a current account.
  - A salary account in addition to other accounts.
- Each account operates independently with its own minimum balance requirements, transaction limits, and debit card.
- Customers can transfer funds between their NovaBank accounts instantly and free of charge.

## 8. Joint Accounts

### 8.1 Types of Joint Account Operation

- **Either or Survivor (E/S):** Either account holder can independently operate the account (recommended for spouses).
- **Both or Survivor (B/S):** Both holders must jointly authorize all transactions.
- **Anyone or Survivor:** Any of the joint holders can operate the account independently (for more than 2 holders).

### 8.2 Joint Account Rules

- Joint accounts are available for savings and current accounts.
- All joint holders must provide KYC documents.
- All joint holders are equally liable for the account.
- In the event of death of one holder, the survivor(s) continue to operate the account.
- Joint holders can nominate a third party as nominee.

## 9. Account Closure

### 9.1 Closure Process

1. Visit a NovaBank branch.
2. Submit a written account closure request form.
3. Surrender the debit card, cheque book, and any other instruments linked to the account.
4. Settle all outstanding charges, penalties, and dues.
5. Choose to transfer the remaining balance to another NovaBank account or receive a demand draft.
6. The account is closed within 3 to 5 working days.

### 9.2 Closure Charges

| Closure Timing | Charges |
|---|---|
| Within 14 days of opening | No charges |
| Within 1 year of opening | INR 500 |
| After 1 year of opening | No charges |

### 9.3 Pre-Closure Checklist

Before closing an account, the customer should:

- Cancel all standing instructions and auto-debits linked to the account.
- Update salary credit instructions with the employer (for salary accounts).
- Transfer linked services (internet banking, mobile banking, UPI) to another account.
- Redeem or transfer any linked reward points.
- Ensure no pending cheques are outstanding.

### 9.4 Closure of Specific Products

- **Salary Account:** If the employer stops salary credits, the account is converted to a regular savings account after 3 months. The minimum balance requirement of the new account type applies.
- **Joint Account:** Closure requires consent of all joint holders (or a death certificate in case of a deceased holder).
- **Minor Account:** Closure requires the guardian's consent and presence.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I open a NovaBank account?
**A:** Visit any NovaBank branch with your PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, proof of address, and two photographs. You can also apply online through the website or mobile app. Account activation takes 1 to 3 working days.

### Q: How do I update my address or phone number?
**A:** Address can be updated via internet banking, mobile app, or branch. Phone number updates require a branch visit or ATM Banking for security reasons.

### Q: What is a dormant account?
**A:** An account with no customer-initiated transactions for 24 months. It can be reactivated by visiting a branch with valid KYC documents.

### Q: How do I add or change my nominee?
**A:** Nomination can be updated through internet banking or by visiting a branch. Submit the appropriate nomination form with the nominee's details.

### Q: Can I have multiple accounts with NovaBank?
**A:** Yes, you can hold multiple accounts of different types. Each account has its own minimum balance and transaction limits.

### Q: How do I close my NovaBank account?
**A:** Visit any branch, submit a closure request, surrender your debit card and cheque book, settle all dues, and choose how to receive the remaining balance. No closure charges apply after 1 year.

### Q: What happens to my salary account if I change jobs?
**A:** If salary credits stop, the account is converted to a regular savings account after 3 months. The applicable minimum balance requirement will apply.

### Q: Can I change my communication preferences?
**A:** Yes, manage preferences through internet banking (Settings > Communication Preferences) or the mobile app (Profile > Notifications). Transaction SMS alerts for security cannot be disabled.

### Q: What does "account frozen" mean?
**A:** A frozen account cannot process any transactions. This may be due to court orders, suspicious activity, or incomplete KYC. Contact NovaBank to understand and resolve the reason.

### Q: How do I reactivate a dormant account?
**A:** Visit a NovaBank branch with valid KYC documents and submit a reactivation request. The account is reactivated within 1 to 2 working days.
