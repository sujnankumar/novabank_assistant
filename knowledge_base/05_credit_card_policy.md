## 2. Definitions

- **Credit Limit:** The maximum amount a cardholder can spend on the credit card at any given time.
- **Billing Cycle:** The monthly period during which transactions are recorded, typically 30 days.
- **Statement Date:** The date on which the monthly credit card statement is generated.
- **Payment Due Date:** The date by which the cardholder must make at least the minimum payment to avoid late fees.
- **Minimum Amount Due (MAD):** The minimum payment required each month, usually 5% of the total outstanding or INR 200, whichever is higher.
- **Finance Charges:** Interest charged on outstanding balances carried beyond the payment due date.
- **Reward Points:** Points earned on eligible transactions that can be redeemed for rewards.

### 6.2 Interest-Free Period

- If the total outstanding amount is paid in full by the payment due date, no finance charges are levied on retail purchases.
- The interest-free period is up to 50 days depending on the transaction date relative to the billing cycle.
- Cash advances do not have an interest-free period; interest accrues from the date of withdrawal.

### 6.3 When Finance Charges Apply

- Finance charges apply on the outstanding balance carried beyond the payment due date.
- If only the minimum amount due (MAD) is paid, finance charges are levied on the remaining outstanding balance from the transaction date.
- Finance charges are compounded monthly.

## 10. Card Security

### 10.1 Card Blocking

- The cardholder can block the card instantly through the NovaBank mobile app, internet banking, or by calling the 24/7 helpline.
- Once blocked, no transactions can be processed on the card.
- A replacement card will be issued within 5-7 working days.

### 10.2 Lost or Stolen Cards

- Report a lost or stolen card immediately by calling the NovaBank helpline at 1800-123-4567 (toll-free).
- The cardholder's liability for unauthorized transactions is limited to INR 0 if reported within 24 hours of the unauthorized transaction.
- If reported between 24 hours and 3 days: liability limited to INR 5,000.
- If reported after 3 days: the cardholder may be liable for unauthorized transactions up to the full amount.

### 10.3 Fraud Protection

- All NovaBank credit cards are equipped with EMV chip technology.
- Two-factor authentication (OTP) is required for all online transactions.
- Real-time transaction alerts are sent via SMS and email.
- NovaBank's fraud monitoring system flags suspicious transaction patterns automatically.

## 13. Dispute Resolution

### 13.1 Transaction Disputes

- Cardholders can raise a dispute for unauthorized transactions, duplicate charges, or merchant-related issues.
- Disputes must be raised within 60 days of the transaction date.
- NovaBank investigates disputes within 7 to 30 working days.
- During investigation, the disputed amount may be temporarily credited to the card account.

### 13.2 Chargeback Process

- If the dispute is valid, NovaBank initiates a chargeback with the merchant's acquiring bank.
- The cardholder is credited with the disputed amount upon successful chargeback.
- If the dispute is not valid, the transaction amount is re-debited with applicable charges.
