## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the rules, procedures, and timelines for transaction processing at NovaBank. It covers successful, failed, pending, and reversed transactions, as well as the dispute resolution process for unauthorized or erroneous transactions.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **Transaction:** Any debit (outflow) or credit (inflow) of funds in a NovaBank account.
- **Transaction ID:** A unique identifier assigned to every transaction (format: TXN followed by a numeric sequence).
- **UPI (Unified Payments Interface):** A real-time payment system enabling instant bank-to-bank transfers.
- **NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer):** An electronic fund transfer system for batch-based transactions.
- **RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement):** A real-time, high-value fund transfer system.
- **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service):** An instant interbank fund transfer service.
- **Chargeback:** A reversal of a card transaction initiated by the cardholder's bank when a dispute is raised.
- **Merchant:** The entity receiving payment in a transaction (e.g., a retailer, service provider, or online platform).

## 4. Transaction Status

Every transaction processed by NovaBank is assigned one of the following statuses:

### 4.1 SUCCESS

- The transaction has been completed and the funds have been transferred.
- The amount has been debited from the sender's account and credited to the recipient's account.
- Successful transactions are irreversible except through the dispute process.

### 4.2 PENDING

- The transaction has been initiated but is awaiting completion.
- Common reasons for pending status:
  - NEFT transactions during batch processing windows
  - Cheque clearing (typically 1-2 working days)
  - Merchant settlement delays
  - System processing during peak hours
- Pending transactions are typically resolved within 1 to 3 working days.
- If a pending transaction is not resolved within 5 working days, the customer should contact NovaBank support.

### 4.3 FAILED

- The transaction could not be completed.
- Common reasons for failure:
  - Insufficient balance
  - Incorrect beneficiary details
  - Bank server downtime
  - Exceeding transaction limits
  - Blocked or restricted account
  - Invalid OTP or authentication failure
- For failed debit transactions: If the amount was debited but the transaction failed, the amount is automatically reversed within 3 to 5 working days.
- If the auto-reversal does not occur within 5 working days, the customer should raise a complaint.

### 4.4 REVERSED

- A transaction that was initially successful but has been reversed.
- Reversal may occur due to:
  - Duplicate transaction detection
  - Merchant-initiated refund
  - Successful dispute/chargeback
  - System error correction
- Reversed amounts are credited back to the original account within 5 to 7 working days.

## 9. Transaction Disputes

### 9.1 When to Raise a Dispute

A customer should raise a dispute if:

- An unauthorized transaction appears on the account
- A duplicate charge is observed
- A transaction amount is incorrect
- A refund was not received within the expected timeframe
- A failed transaction resulted in a debit but no reversal
- A merchant delivered goods/services different from what was paid for

### 9.2 How to Raise a Dispute

1. **Mobile App / Internet Banking:** Navigate to the transaction and select "Raise Dispute."
2. **Customer Service Helpline:** Call 1800-123-4567 and provide transaction details.
3. **Branch Visit:** Submit a written dispute form at any NovaBank branch.
4. **Email:** Send dispute details to support@novabank.fictional (include transaction ID, date, amount, and description of the issue).

### 9.3 Information Required for Dispute

- Transaction ID or reference number
- Transaction date and amount
- Merchant name (if applicable)
- Description of the issue
- Supporting documents (screenshots, receipts, communication with merchant)

### 9.4 Dispute Investigation Timeline

| Dispute Type | Investigation Period | Resolution Period |
|---|---|---|
| Unauthorized transaction | 7 working days | 10 working days |
| Duplicate charge | 5 working days | 7 working days |
| Failed transaction (debit without credit) | 3 working days | 5 working days |
| Merchant dispute | 15 working days | 30 working days |
| International transaction dispute | 30 working days | 45 working days |

### 9.5 Provisional Credit

- For unauthorized transactions reported within 3 days, NovaBank may provide a provisional credit within 10 working days while the investigation is ongoing.
- If the dispute is found to be invalid, the provisional credit is reversed with prior notice to the customer.

## 10. UPI Transaction Disputes

### 10.1 Common UPI Issues

- **Amount debited but not credited to beneficiary:** The amount is automatically reversed within 3 to 5 working days. If not, raise a dispute.
- **Wrong UPI ID:** Transactions to an incorrect UPI ID can only be reversed if the recipient agrees. NovaBank will facilitate communication with the recipient's bank.
- **Duplicate UPI transaction:** Raise a dispute within 48 hours for quick resolution.

### 10.2 UPI Dispute Process

1. Check the transaction status in the UPI app (it may show "PENDING" or "DEEMED").
2. If the status shows "FAILED" but money was debited, wait 3 working days for auto-reversal.
3. If auto-reversal does not occur, raise a dispute through the NovaBank app or helpline.
4. NovaBank will coordinate with NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) and the beneficiary bank.

## 11. Card Transaction Disputes

### 11.1 Chargeback Process

1. The customer raises a dispute for an unauthorized or erroneous card transaction.
2. NovaBank initiates a chargeback request with the merchant's acquiring bank.
3. The merchant is given an opportunity to respond (typically 10 to 30 days).
4. If the chargeback is successful, the amount is credited to the customer's account.
5. If the chargeback is denied, NovaBank informs the customer with reasons.

### 11.2 Chargeback Eligibility

Chargebacks can be initiated for:

- Unauthorized transactions (card not present fraud)
- Duplicate charges
- Non-receipt of goods/services
- Goods/services significantly different from what was described
- Cancelled recurring subscriptions that continue to be charged

Chargebacks must be raised within 60 days of the transaction date.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What should I do if my transaction failed but money was debited?
**A:** Wait 3 to 5 working days for automatic reversal. If the amount is not reversed, raise a dispute through the NovaBank app, helpline (1800-123-4567), or by visiting a branch.

### Q: How can I check my transaction history?
**A:** Log in to NovaBank internet banking or the mobile app. You can view the last 12 months of transactions and filter by date, type, or amount.

### Q: What are the transaction limits for UPI?
**A:** UPI transactions are limited to INR 1,00,000 per transaction for savings accounts and INR 2,00,000 for current accounts.

### Q: How do I raise a transaction dispute?
**A:** You can raise a dispute through the NovaBank mobile app, internet banking, by calling 1800-123-4567, or by visiting any branch. You will need the transaction ID, date, amount, and a description of the issue.

### Q: How long does a dispute investigation take?
**A:** The investigation period ranges from 3 working days (failed transactions) to 45 working days (international disputes). Most domestic disputes are resolved within 7 to 10 working days.

### Q: What is a pending transaction?
**A:** A pending transaction has been initiated but is not yet completed. This can happen due to batch processing, cheque clearing, or merchant settlement delays. It typically resolves within 1 to 3 working days.

### Q: Can I get my money back for an unauthorized transaction?
**A:** Yes. Report the unauthorized transaction immediately. If reported within 3 days, NovaBank may provide a provisional credit within 10 working days while investigating.

### Q: How do I download my account statement?
**A:** Log in to internet banking or the mobile app, navigate to Statements, select the date range, and download in PDF or CSV format.

### Q: What is the difference between NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS?
**A:** NEFT processes in batches (within 30 minutes), RTGS is real-time for high-value transfers (minimum INR 2,00,000), and IMPS is instant for any amount up to INR 5,00,000. All three are available 24x7 at NovaBank.

### Q: Are there charges for fund transfers?
**A:** NEFT and RTGS transfers are free at NovaBank. IMPS is free up to INR 1,00,000; a nominal INR 5 charge applies for amounts above INR 1,00,000.
