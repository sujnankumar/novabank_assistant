## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the eligibility criteria, interest rates, documentation requirements, and repayment terms for NovaBank vehicle loans. These loans are designed to help customers purchase new two-wheelers and cars from authorized dealers.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **On-Road Price:** The total cost of the vehicle including ex-showroom price, registration, insurance, road tax, and other applicable charges.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio:** The percentage of the vehicle's on-road price that the bank will finance through the loan.
- **Down Payment:** The portion of the vehicle cost that the borrower pays upfront, not covered by the loan.
- **Hypothecation:** The vehicle purchased through the loan is hypothecated (pledged) to NovaBank as security until the loan is fully repaid.
- **Proforma Invoice:** A preliminary invoice from the vehicle dealer stating the vehicle details and price.

## 11. Application and Approval Process

1. **Vehicle Selection:** The customer selects the vehicle and obtains a quotation or proforma invoice from the authorized dealer.
2. **Application Submission:** The customer submits the loan application with all required documents.
3. **Document Verification:** NovaBank verifies identity, income, and vehicle documentation.
4. **Credit Assessment:** Credit score, income adequacy, and existing obligations are evaluated.
5. **Approval:** If all criteria are met, a sanction letter is issued.
6. **Down Payment:** The customer pays the down payment to the dealer.
7. **Agreement Execution:** The customer signs the loan agreement and hypothecation deed.
8. **Disbursement:** The loan amount is disbursed directly to the dealer's account.
9. **Vehicle Registration:** The vehicle is registered with NovaBank as the hypothecation holder in the RC (Registration Certificate).

Standard processing time: 2 to 5 working days.

---

## 12. Disbursement

- The loan amount is disbursed directly to the authorized dealer's bank account. It is not disbursed to the borrower's personal account.
- Disbursement occurs after the loan agreement is executed and the down payment is confirmed.
- Vehicle delivery should occur within 7 days of disbursement.

## 13. EMI and Repayment

- EMIs begin 30 days after the date of disbursement.
- EMIs must be paid via auto-debit from a NovaBank account or NACH mandate.
- EMIs are calculated on a reducing balance basis.

For a Car Loan Standard of INR 8,00,000 at 9.25% p.a. for 5 years:

- Approximate EMI: INR 16,680
- Total Interest Payable: INR 2,00,800
- Total Amount Payable: INR 10,00,800

## 14. Prepayment and Foreclosure

### 14.1 Prepayment

- Partial prepayment is allowed after a minimum of 6 EMI payments.
- Prepayment charge: 2% of the prepayment amount.
- Minimum prepayment: INR 10,000 or one EMI amount, whichever is higher.
- Maximum 2 prepayments per financial year.

### 14.2 Foreclosure

- Full foreclosure is allowed after a minimum of 12 EMI payments.
- Foreclosure charge: 3% of outstanding principal if within 24 months; 1% if after 24 months.
- A written request must be submitted at least 10 working days in advance.
- Upon foreclosure, NovaBank will issue a No Objection Certificate (NOC) for removing the hypothecation from the RC.

---

## 15. Hypothecation and Ownership

- The vehicle is hypothecated to NovaBank for the duration of the loan.
- The borrower is the registered owner but cannot sell, transfer, or modify the vehicle without NovaBank's written consent.
- Upon full repayment (including foreclosure), NovaBank issues an NOC to remove the hypothecation endorsement from the RC.
- If the borrower defaults, NovaBank reserves the right to repossess the vehicle through legal proceedings.

---

## 16. Insurance Requirements

- Comprehensive vehicle insurance is mandatory for the entire loan tenure.
- The insurance policy must name NovaBank as the hypothecation holder.
- The borrower must renew the insurance annually and provide proof of renewal to NovaBank.
- Lapse of insurance during the loan tenure is a breach of the loan agreement.

---

## 17. Late Payment and Default

- A late payment fee of INR 500 plus 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount is charged.
- If an EMI remains unpaid for more than 90 days, the account is classified as NPA.
- Continued default may result in:
  - Vehicle repossession through legal proceedings
  - Legal recovery action
  - Credit score impact for the borrower
- NovaBank will send reminders via SMS, email, and phone before initiating any recovery action.

## 18. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I get a vehicle loan for a used car?
**A:** NovaBank currently offers vehicle loans only for brand-new vehicles purchased from authorized dealerships. Used vehicle financing is not available at this time.

### Q: What is the minimum down payment for a car loan?
**A:** The minimum down payment is 15% of the on-road price for the Car Loan Standard and 20% for the Car Loan Premium. For two-wheelers, it is 10%.

### Q: What is the interest rate for a car loan?
**A:** The interest rate is 9.25% p.a. for the Car Loan Standard and 8.75% p.a. for the Car Loan Premium.

### Q: What documents are needed for a vehicle loan?
**A:** Common documents include PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Salary Slips, Bank Statements, Vehicle Proforma Invoice, and Driving License. See Section 10 for the full list.

### Q: Can I prepay my vehicle loan?
**A:** Yes, partial prepayment is allowed after 6 EMI payments with a 2% charge. Full foreclosure is allowed after 12 EMI payments.

### Q: What happens to the vehicle if I default on the loan?
**A:** Continued default may lead to vehicle repossession through legal proceedings, legal recovery action, and negative credit reporting.

### Q: How quickly can I get a vehicle loan approved?
**A:** The standard processing time is 2 to 5 working days.

### Q: Do I need a driving license to apply for a car loan?
**A:** Yes, a valid driving license is required for car loan applications (Car Loan Standard and Car Loan Premium).

### Q: Are electric vehicles eligible for NovaBank vehicle loans?
**A:** Yes, both electric two-wheelers and electric cars from recognized manufacturers are eligible. EV purchases may qualify for a 0.25% interest rate concession.

### Q: Who receives the loan amount -- me or the dealer?
**A:** The loan amount is disbursed directly to the authorized dealer's bank account, not to the borrower's personal account.
