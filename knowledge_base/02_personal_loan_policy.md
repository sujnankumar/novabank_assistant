## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the eligibility criteria, interest rates, fees, repayment terms, and application procedures for all NovaBank personal loan products. Personal loans from NovaBank are unsecured loans available for a wide range of personal financial needs including medical expenses, travel, home renovation, weddings, debt consolidation, and other legitimate purposes.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **Unsecured Loan:** A loan that does not require any collateral or security from the borrower.
- **EMI (Equated Monthly Installment):** A fixed monthly payment comprising both principal and interest components.
- **Processing Fee:** A non-refundable fee charged for processing the loan application.
- **Prepayment:** Paying a portion of the outstanding principal before the scheduled date.
- **Foreclosure:** Repaying the entire outstanding loan amount before the end of the loan tenure.
- **Disbursement:** The transfer of the sanctioned loan amount to the borrower's account.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### 4.1 Age Requirements

- **Personal Loan Lite:** 21 to 60 years at the time of application.
- **Personal Loan Plus:** 23 to 58 years at the time of application.
- **Personal Loan Premium:** 25 to 55 years at the time of application.
- **Instant Personal Loan:** 21 to 55 years at the time of application.

The loan tenure must not extend beyond the applicant's maximum age limit for the respective product.

### 4.2 Income Requirements

- **Personal Loan Lite:** Minimum monthly income of INR 20,000.
- **Personal Loan Plus:** Minimum monthly income of INR 40,000.
- **Personal Loan Premium:** Minimum monthly income of INR 75,000.
- **Instant Personal Loan:** Minimum monthly income of INR 15,000.

For salaried individuals, income is validated through salary slips and bank statements. For self-employed individuals, IT Returns and business financials are assessed.

### 4.3 Credit Score Requirements

- **Personal Loan Lite:** Minimum credit score of 650.
- **Personal Loan Plus:** Minimum credit score of 720.
- **Personal Loan Premium:** Minimum credit score of 760.
- **Instant Personal Loan:** Minimum credit score of 630.

Applicants with higher credit scores may be offered preferential interest rates. NovaBank checks credit history from all major credit bureaus.

### 4.4 Employment

- Salaried employees must have a minimum of 1 year of total work experience and at least 3 months with the current employer.
- Self-employed individuals must have at least 2 years of business continuity.
- The Instant Personal Loan is available exclusively to existing NovaBank customers with a minimum account relationship of 6 months.

### 4.5 Existing Obligations

- The total EMI burden (including the proposed loan) should not exceed 50% of the applicant's monthly income.
- Applicants with existing defaults or write-offs in the last 12 months will not be eligible.

## 9. Application Process

### 9.1 Standard Application (Lite, Plus, Premium)

1. **Inquiry:** Customer inquires about personal loan options through branches, the website, or the helpline.
2. **Application Submission:** Customer fills out the application form and submits all required documents.
3. **Document Verification:** NovaBank verifies all submitted documents.
4. **Credit Assessment:** Credit score is checked and income-to-EMI ratio is evaluated.
5. **Approval:** If all criteria are met, the loan is approved with a formal sanction letter.
6. **Acceptance:** Customer reviews and signs the loan agreement.
7. **Disbursement:** The loan amount (minus processing fee) is credited to the customer's NovaBank account.

Standard processing time: 3 to 7 working days.

### 9.2 Instant Personal Loan Application

1. **Pre-qualification:** NovaBank identifies eligible customers based on account history, credit score, and relationship.
2. **Offer:** An instant loan offer is presented to the customer via the mobile app, internet banking, or SMS.
3. **Acceptance:** Customer accepts the offer and confirms the desired amount and tenure.
4. **e-KYC and e-Sign:** Customer completes e-KYC verification and digitally signs the agreement.
5. **Disbursement:** The loan amount is credited within 2 hours of acceptance.

## 10. Disbursement

## 11. EMI and Repayment

- EMIs are calculated using the reducing balance method.
- All EMIs must be paid via auto-debit from a NovaBank account or NACH mandate.
- The EMI date is fixed on the same day each month as specified in the loan agreement.
- Failure to maintain sufficient balance for auto-debit on the EMI date will result in late payment charges.

## 12. Prepayment and Foreclosure

### 12.1 Prepayment

- Partial prepayment is allowed after a minimum of 3 EMI payments.
- A prepayment charge of 3% of the prepayment amount is applicable.
- Minimum prepayment amount is INR 10,000 or 10% of the outstanding principal, whichever is higher.
- A maximum of 2 prepayments are allowed per financial year.

### 12.2 Foreclosure

- Full foreclosure is allowed after a minimum of 6 EMI payments.
- Foreclosure charges:
  - Within 12 months of disbursement: 4% of outstanding principal.
  - After 12 months of disbursement: 2% of outstanding principal.
- The customer must submit a written foreclosure request at least 10 working days before the intended date.

---

## 13. Late Payment and Default

- A late payment fee of INR 500 plus 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount is charged.
- If an EMI remains unpaid for more than 90 days, the account is classified as NPA.
- Continued default may result in legal recovery proceedings.
- All overdue and default information is reported to credit bureaus, negatively impacting the borrower's credit score.
- NovaBank will send reminders via SMS, email, and phone before initiating recovery action.
