## 4. Complaint Handling Process

### 4.1 Filing a Complaint

1. **Choose a channel:** Contact NovaBank via any of the support channels listed in Section 2.
2. **Provide details:** Share your customer ID, account number, and a clear description of the issue. Include relevant transaction IDs, dates, and amounts.
3. **Receive reference number:** A unique complaint reference number (CRN) is issued for tracking.
4. **Confirmation:** An acknowledgment email/SMS is sent within 1 hour of complaint registration.

### 4.2 Investigation and Resolution

1. **Assignment:** The complaint is assigned to the relevant department based on category.
2. **Investigation:** The team reviews transaction logs, system records, and customer communication.
3. **Resolution:** The issue is resolved and the customer is informed via email/SMS/phone.
4. **Closure:** The complaint is closed after the customer confirms satisfaction. If no response is received within 7 days of resolution notification, the complaint is auto-closed.

### 4.3 Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

| Priority | SLA | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | 24 hours | Fraud, unauthorized transactions, account compromise |
| **High** | 3 working days | Failed transactions, card blocking, login issues |
| **Medium** | 7 working days | Fee disputes, account service requests |
| **Low** | 15 working days | General inquiries, product information, feedback |

## 5. Escalation Process

If a complaint is not resolved within the expected timeline, customers can escalate through the following hierarchy:

### Level 1: Customer Service Representative

- **How:** Call 1800-123-4567 and request escalation, or use the chat/email channel.
- **Response Time:** 1 to 3 working days.

### Level 2: Branch Manager / Department Head

- **How:** Visit the branch and request to speak with the branch manager. Alternatively, email escalation@novabank.fictional with the complaint reference number.
- **Response Time:** 3 to 5 working days.

### Level 3: Customer Grievance Officer

- **How:** Email grievance.officer@novabank.fictional with full details and complaint reference number.
- **Response Time:** 7 working days.
- The Grievance Officer is a designated senior official responsible for addressing unresolved complaints.

### Level 4: Banking Ombudsman (External)

- **When:** If the complaint remains unresolved after 30 days, or if the customer is not satisfied with the resolution provided by Level 3.
- **How:** File a complaint with the Banking Ombudsman (this is a regulatory body external to NovaBank).
- **Note:** NovaBank cooperates fully with the Banking Ombudsman's office in resolving escalated complaints.

## 8. Loan-Related Support

### 8.1 Support Available

- **Application Status:** Track loan application status through the mobile app, internet banking, or helpline.
- **EMI Queries:** Check EMI schedule, upcoming EMIs, and payment history.
- **Prepayment/Foreclosure:** Request prepayment or foreclosure through the branch or internet banking.
- **Rate Renegotiation:** Eligible customers can request interest rate review after 12 EMI payments.
- **Loan Closure Certificate:** Issued within 3 to 5 working days of full loan repayment.
- **NOC (No Objection Certificate):** Issued for vehicle loans and home loans within 5 working days of loan closure.

### 8.2 EMI-Related Issues

- If an EMI auto-debit fails, the customer is notified via SMS and email.
- The customer has 3 days to make the payment manually to avoid late payment charges.
- If the issue is with the auto-debit mandate, the customer should update the mandate through the branch.
