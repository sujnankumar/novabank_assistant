## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the features, eligibility criteria, interest rates, fees, transaction limits, and account management procedures for NovaBank savings accounts. Savings accounts are the most fundamental banking product, designed to encourage personal savings while providing convenient access to funds.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **Minimum Balance:** The minimum amount that must be maintained in the account at all times to avoid penalty charges.
- **Average Monthly Balance (AMB):** The average of the daily closing balances in the account over a calendar month.
- **Dormant Account:** An account with no customer-initiated transactions for a continuous period of 24 months.
- **Nomination:** The designation of a person who will receive the account balance in the event of the account holder's death.
- **KYC (Know Your Customer):** The identity verification process required for opening a bank account.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### 4.1 Basic Savings Account

- Indian resident aged 18 years or above.
- Valid KYC documents (PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, proof of address).
- No minimum income requirement.

### 4.2 Premium Savings Account

- Indian resident aged 18 years or above.
- Valid KYC documents.
- Minimum monthly income of INR 30,000 (verified through salary slips or bank statements).
- Existing NovaBank customers with 6+ months of relationship may be upgraded to Premium.

### 4.3 Zero Balance Savings Account

- Indian resident aged 18 years or above.
- Valid KYC documents.
- No minimum income requirement.
- No minimum balance requirement.
- Ideal for students, freelancers, and individuals who do not want to maintain a minimum balance.

## 6. Minimum Balance Requirements

| Product | Minimum Balance | Penalty for Non-Maintenance |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Savings Account | INR 1,000 (AMB) | INR 100 per month |
| Premium Savings Account | INR 25,000 (AMB) | INR 500 per month |
| Zero Balance Savings Account | INR 0 | No penalty |

- The minimum balance is assessed as the Average Monthly Balance (AMB).
- If the AMB falls below the required minimum in any month, the penalty is automatically debited in the following month.
- Repeated non-maintenance for 3 consecutive months may result in account downgrade (e.g., Premium to Basic).

## 7. Account Opening

### 7.1 Required Documents

- PAN Card (mandatory for all accounts)
- Aadhaar Card or other acceptable proof of identity
- Proof of address (Aadhaar, passport, utility bill, or rent agreement)
- Two passport-size photographs
- For Premium Savings Account: income proof (salary slips or bank statements)

### 7.2 Opening Process

1. Visit a NovaBank branch or apply online through the NovaBank website or mobile app.
2. Fill out the account opening form.
3. Submit KYC documents.
4. Complete in-person verification (IPV) or video KYC for online applications.
5. Make the initial deposit (not applicable for Zero Balance accounts).
6. Receive your account number, debit card, and internet banking credentials within 3 to 5 working days.

### 7.3 Initial Deposit

- **Basic Savings Account:** Minimum INR 1,000.
- **Premium Savings Account:** Minimum INR 25,000.
- **Zero Balance Savings Account:** No initial deposit required.

## 8. Deposits

- Cash deposits can be made at any NovaBank branch or designated ATM.
- Cash deposits up to INR 50,000 per day are accepted without additional documentation.
- Cash deposits above INR 50,000 in a single day require PAN Card details.
- Cheque deposits are accepted at branches and via drop boxes at select ATMs.
- Electronic transfers (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI) can be received without any limit.

---

## 9. Withdrawals

### 9.1 ATM Withdrawals

- NovaBank ATM withdrawals: Free and unlimited at NovaBank ATMs.
- Other bank ATMs: 5 free transactions per month at non-NovaBank ATMs. Beyond this, INR 20 per transaction is charged.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: INR 25,000 for Basic and Zero Balance accounts; INR 50,000 for Premium accounts.

### 9.2 Branch Withdrawals

- Cash withdrawals at branches are available during banking hours.
- Withdrawals above INR 50,000 may require prior notice of 1 working day.

## 12. Debit Card

- A free debit card is issued with every savings account.
- **Basic and Zero Balance Accounts:** NovaBank Classic Debit Card (daily POS limit: INR 1,00,000).
- **Premium Accounts:** NovaBank Gold Debit Card (daily POS limit: INR 2,50,000).
- Debit card replacement fee: INR 200 (first replacement free for Premium accounts).
- International debit card usage can be enabled/disabled through the mobile app.

## 13. Dormant Accounts

- An account is classified as dormant if there are no customer-initiated transactions (debit or credit) for a continuous period of 24 months.
- Dormant accounts are restricted from debits until reactivated.
- To reactivate a dormant account, the customer must visit a NovaBank branch with valid KYC documents and submit a reactivation request.
- Interest continues to accrue on dormant accounts.
- Accounts dormant for more than 10 years may be transferred to the regulatory authority as unclaimed deposits.

---

## 14. Account Closure

- The account holder can request account closure at any NovaBank branch.
- A written closure request form must be submitted.
- All outstanding charges, penalties, and dues must be settled before closure.
- The remaining balance is either transferred to another account or provided as a demand draft.
- Account closure within 14 days of opening: no charges.
- Account closure within 1 year of opening: INR 500 closure charge.
- Account closure after 1 year: no closure charge.
- The debit card linked to the account is automatically deactivated upon closure.

---

## 15. Nomination

- Account holders can nominate one person to receive the account balance in the event of the account holder's death.
- Nomination can be registered at the time of account opening or updated later through a branch visit or internet banking.
- Nomination does not override a legal will or succession laws. It is a convenience facility for immediate access.
- Multiple nominees are not permitted for individual savings accounts.

---

## 16. Joint Accounts

- NovaBank savings accounts can be opened jointly with one other individual.
- Joint accounts can be operated as:
  - **Either or Survivor:** Either account holder can operate the account independently.
  - **Both or Survivor:** Both account holders must authorize all transactions jointly.
- Joint accounts are not available for the Zero Balance Savings Account.

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What savings account options does NovaBank offer?
**A:** NovaBank offers three savings accounts: Basic Savings (INR 1,000 minimum balance, 3.00% interest), Premium Savings (INR 25,000 minimum balance, 4.00% interest), and Zero Balance Savings (no minimum balance, 2.50% interest).

### Q: What is the minimum balance for a NovaBank savings account?
**A:** INR 1,000 for the Basic Savings Account, INR 25,000 for the Premium Savings Account, and INR 0 for the Zero Balance Savings Account.

### Q: What is the interest rate on savings accounts?
**A:** 3.00% p.a. for Basic, 4.00% p.a. for Premium, and 2.50% p.a. for Zero Balance accounts. Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited quarterly.

### Q: How do I open a savings account?
**A:** Visit a NovaBank branch with your PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, proof of address, and two photographs. You can also apply online through the NovaBank website or mobile app.

### Q: What happens if I don't maintain the minimum balance?
**A:** A penalty of INR 100 per month (Basic) or INR 500 per month (Premium) is charged. Zero Balance accounts have no penalty.

### Q: How do I close my savings account?
**A:** Visit a NovaBank branch and submit a written closure request. Settle all outstanding charges. No closure fee applies after 1 year.

### Q: What is a dormant account?
**A:** An account with no customer-initiated transactions for 24 continuous months is classified as dormant. It can be reactivated by visiting a branch with KYC documents.

### Q: Can I have multiple savings accounts with NovaBank?
**A:** Yes, you can hold multiple savings accounts of different types. However, each account is subject to its own minimum balance and transaction limit requirements.

### Q: What are the ATM withdrawal limits?
**A:** INR 25,000 per day for Basic and Zero Balance accounts, and INR 50,000 per day for Premium accounts.

### Q: How do I update my nomination?
**A:** Nomination can be updated by visiting a NovaBank branch or through internet banking under the account management section.
