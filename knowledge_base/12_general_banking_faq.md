## 2. Account-Related Questions

### Q: What is a savings account?
**A:** A savings account is a deposit account held at a bank that allows you to safely store your money while earning interest. It is designed for individuals who want to save money and earn a modest return. NovaBank offers three types of savings accounts: Basic Savings (3.00% p.a. interest, INR 1,000 minimum balance), Premium Savings (4.00% p.a., INR 25,000 minimum balance), and Zero Balance Savings (2.50% p.a., no minimum balance).

### Q: What is a current account?
**A:** A current account is a bank account designed for businesses and professionals who need to make frequent transactions. Unlike savings accounts, current accounts typically do not earn interest but offer higher transaction limits. NovaBank offers the Business Current Account (INR 10,000 minimum balance, 500 transactions/month) and Premium Current Account (INR 50,000 minimum balance, 1,000 transactions/month).

### Q: What is a salary account?
**A:** A salary account is a type of savings account opened by employers for disbursing employee salaries. It typically has zero minimum balance requirements and additional benefits such as fee waivers. At NovaBank, salary accounts are opened through employer partnerships and offer similar benefits to the Zero Balance Savings Account.

### Q: What is the difference between a savings account and a current account?
**A:** The key differences are:
- **Interest:** Savings accounts earn interest (2.50% to 4.00% p.a. at NovaBank); current accounts typically do not.
- **Transaction Limits:** Current accounts have much higher transaction limits (500-1,000/month vs. 30-100/month for savings).
- **Purpose:** Savings accounts are for personal savings; current accounts are for business operations.
- **Minimum Balance:** Current accounts may have higher minimum balance requirements.

### Q: What is a joint account?
**A:** A joint account is a bank account shared by two or more individuals. NovaBank offers joint accounts with two modes of operation: "Either or Survivor" (either holder can operate independently) and "Both or Survivor" (both must authorize transactions). Joint accounts are useful for spouses, business partners, or family members.

## 3. Fixed Deposit Questions

### Q: What is a fixed deposit (FD)?
**A:** A fixed deposit is a savings instrument where you deposit a lump sum of money for a fixed period (tenure) at a predetermined interest rate. The money is locked in for the chosen tenure, and you earn guaranteed returns. FDs are considered one of the safest investment options. NovaBank offers Regular FD (6.50% p.a.), Tax Saver FD (6.80% p.a.), Senior Citizen FD (7.25% p.a.), and Flexi FD (6.25% p.a.).

### Q: What is the difference between a cumulative and non-cumulative FD?
**A:** 
- **Cumulative FD:** Interest is compounded quarterly and paid along with the principal at maturity. Best for long-term savings.
- **Non-Cumulative FD:** Interest is paid out at regular intervals (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or annually). Best for those who need periodic income.

### Q: What happens when my FD matures?
**A:** At maturity, the principal plus accrued interest (for cumulative FDs) is credited to your linked savings account. If you have not given any maturity instructions, the FD is automatically renewed at the prevailing interest rate for the same tenure.

### Q: Can I break my FD before maturity?
**A:** Yes, for Regular and Senior Citizen FDs, premature withdrawal is allowed with a penalty of 1.00% deducted from the applicable interest rate. Tax Saver FDs have a mandatory 5-year lock-in and cannot be broken early.

## 4. Loan-Related Questions

### Q: What is a loan?
**A:** A loan is a sum of money borrowed from a bank that must be repaid over a specified period along with interest. NovaBank offers Home Loans (starting from 8.25% p.a.), Personal Loans (starting from 10.25% p.a.), Education Loans (starting from 9.50% p.a.), and Vehicle Loans (starting from 8.75% p.a.).

### Q: What is an EMI?
**A:** EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed monthly payment you make to repay a loan. Each EMI consists of two components: principal repayment and interest payment. In the early years, the interest component is larger, and over time, the principal component increases. For example, for a home loan of INR 50 lakhs at 8.50% for 20 years, the approximate EMI would be INR 43,391.

### Q: How is EMI calculated?
**A:** EMI is calculated using the formula:
EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)
Where P = Principal (loan amount), r = monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12), n = number of monthly installments. NovaBank provides an EMI calculator on the website and mobile app for easy estimation.

### Q: What is a credit score?
**A:** A credit score is a numerical rating (typically between 300 and 900) that indicates your creditworthiness. It is based on your credit history, repayment behavior, outstanding debts, credit utilization, and the length of your credit history. A higher score indicates better creditworthiness and improves your chances of loan approval and favorable interest rates. NovaBank's minimum credit score requirements range from 600 to 760 depending on the loan product.

### Q: What is the difference between a secured and unsecured loan?
**A:**
- **Secured Loan:** Backed by collateral (an asset pledged as security). If the borrower defaults, the bank can seize the collateral. Examples at NovaBank: Home Loans (property as collateral), Vehicle Loans (vehicle as collateral). These typically have lower interest rates.
- **Unsecured Loan:** Not backed by collateral. The bank relies on the borrower's creditworthiness. Examples at NovaBank: Personal Loans. These typically have higher interest rates due to the higher risk.

### Q: What is loan tenure?
**A:** Tenure is the total duration over which a loan is repaid. It is usually expressed in years or months. Longer tenure means lower EMIs but higher total interest paid. Shorter tenure means higher EMIs but lower total interest. NovaBank's loan tenures range from 3 years (Instant Personal Loan) to 30 years (Home Loan).

### Q: What is a processing fee?
**A:** A processing fee is a one-time charge levied by the bank for processing a loan application. It covers administrative costs such as document verification, credit assessment, and legal review. At NovaBank, processing fees range from 0.35% to 2.50% of the loan amount depending on the product.

### Q: What is prepayment?
**A:** Prepayment is the act of paying a portion of the outstanding loan principal before the scheduled EMI date. It reduces the outstanding principal and can shorten the loan tenure or reduce future EMIs. Some loans may have prepayment charges (e.g., 2% to 3% for personal loans at NovaBank). Home loans at NovaBank do not have prepayment charges for floating-rate loans.

### Q: What is foreclosure?
**A:** Foreclosure (in the loan context) means repaying the entire outstanding loan amount before the end of the loan tenure. It closes the loan account completely. Some products may have foreclosure charges. NovaBank does not charge foreclosure penalties for floating-rate home loans and education loans.

### Q: What is a co-applicant?
**A:** A co-applicant is a person who applies for a loan jointly with the primary borrower. The co-applicant's income and credit score are considered for loan eligibility. Both the borrower and co-applicant are equally liable for repayment. Common co-applicants include spouses, parents, or siblings. Co-applicants are mandatory for all NovaBank education loans.

### Q: What is loan-to-value (LTV) ratio?
**A:** LTV ratio is the percentage of the asset's value that the bank will finance through the loan. For example, if a property is worth INR 1 crore and the bank offers an LTV of 80%, the maximum loan amount is INR 80 lakhs. The remaining 20% must be paid by the borrower as a down payment. NovaBank's LTV ranges from 80% to 90% depending on the product.

## 5. Interest Rate Questions

### Q: What is an interest rate?
**A:** An interest rate is the percentage charged by the bank on the borrowed amount (for loans) or paid to the depositor on the deposited amount (for savings/FDs). It is usually expressed as an annual percentage (per annum or p.a.). For example, NovaBank's Home Loan Standard has an interest rate of 8.50% p.a.

### Q: What is the difference between a fixed rate and a floating rate?
**A:**
- **Fixed Rate:** The interest rate remains the same throughout the loan tenure. Your EMI will not change. NovaBank personal loans are offered at fixed rates.
- **Floating Rate:** The interest rate can change during the loan tenure based on market conditions and bank policy. Your EMI may increase or decrease.

### Q: What is principal?
**A:** Principal is the original amount of money borrowed (for loans) or deposited (for FDs). In a loan, each EMI payment reduces the outstanding principal. Interest is calculated on the remaining principal balance (reducing balance method). For example, if you take a loan of INR 10 lakhs, that INR 10 lakhs is the principal.

### Q: What is compound interest?
**A:** Compound interest is interest calculated on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from previous periods. NovaBank savings accounts compound interest quarterly, and FDs compound interest quarterly for cumulative deposits. Compounding leads to higher effective returns compared to simple interest.

### Q: What is the reducing balance method?
**A:** Under the reducing balance method, interest is calculated on the outstanding principal balance, which decreases with each EMI payment. This means you pay less interest over time compared to the flat-rate method. NovaBank uses the reducing balance method for all loan EMI calculations.

## 6. Transaction-Related Questions

### Q: What is a transaction reference number?
**A:** A transaction reference number (or Transaction ID) is a unique alphanumeric code assigned to every financial transaction. It is used to track, verify, and reference the transaction. At NovaBank, transaction IDs follow the format TXN followed by a numeric sequence (e.g., TXN00001). You should note this number for any transaction disputes.

### Q: What is a failed transaction?
**A:** A failed transaction is one that could not be completed due to reasons such as insufficient balance, incorrect beneficiary details, system errors, or authentication failure. If money was debited for a failed transaction, it is usually automatically reversed within 3 to 5 working days. If the reversal does not happen, contact NovaBank support.

### Q: What is a pending transaction?
**A:** A pending transaction has been initiated but is not yet fully processed. This may happen due to batch processing (NEFT), cheque clearing, or merchant settlement delays. Pending transactions are typically resolved within 1 to 3 working days. If a transaction remains pending beyond 5 working days, contact NovaBank support.

### Q: What is UPI?
**A:** UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is a real-time payment system that enables instant bank-to-bank transfers using a UPI ID or QR code. It is available 24x7 and is free for most transactions. NovaBank supports UPI payments through the NovaBank mobile app and other UPI-enabled apps.

### Q: What is the difference between NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS?
**A:**
| Feature | NEFT | RTGS | IMPS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed | Batch (within 30 min) | Real-time | Instant |
| Minimum Amount | INR 1 | INR 2,00,000 | INR 1 |
| Maximum Amount | INR 10,00,000 | INR 10,00,000 | INR 5,00,000 |
| Availability | 24x7 | 24x7 | 24x7 |
| Charges at NovaBank | Free | Free | Free up to INR 1L |

### Q: What is a chargeback?
**A:** A chargeback is a reversal of a card transaction initiated by the cardholder's bank (NovaBank) when a dispute is raised. If a customer disputes a credit or debit card transaction, NovaBank can initiate a chargeback with the merchant's bank. If successful, the transaction amount is refunded to the customer.

## 7. Account Management Questions

### Q: What is a minimum balance?
**A:** The minimum balance is the amount that must be maintained in a savings or current account at all times (measured as Average Monthly Balance). If the balance falls below this threshold, a penalty is charged. NovaBank's minimum balances are: Basic Savings - INR 1,000; Premium Savings - INR 25,000; Zero Balance - INR 0; Business Current - INR 10,000; Premium Current - INR 50,000.

### Q: What is a nominee?
**A:** A nominee is a person designated by the account holder to receive the account balance or FD proceeds in the event of the account holder's death. Nomination is a convenience facility for immediate access and does not override a legal will. At NovaBank, you can register or change your nominee through internet banking or at a branch.

### Q: What is KYC?
**A:** KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It is a mandatory identity verification process required for opening bank accounts and availing financial services. KYC documents typically include PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, proof of address, and a photograph. NovaBank requires periodic KYC updates (re-KYC) every 2 to 10 years depending on the customer's risk profile.

### Q: What is a dormant account?
**A:** A dormant account is one that has had no customer-initiated transactions for 24 consecutive months. Debit transactions are blocked on dormant accounts, though credits continue to be received. To reactivate, visit a NovaBank branch with valid KYC documents.

### Q: What is an account statement?
**A:** An account statement is a summary of all transactions (credits and debits) in your account over a specific period. It includes transaction dates, descriptions, amounts, and the closing balance. NovaBank generates monthly statements and makes them available through internet banking, the mobile app, and email.

## 8. Credit Card Questions

### Q: What is a credit limit?
**A:** The credit limit is the maximum amount you can spend on your credit card. It is determined by the bank based on your income, credit score, and repayment history. NovaBank credit card limits range from INR 2,00,000 (Classic) to INR 15,00,000 (Platinum).

### Q: What is the billing cycle?
**A:** The billing cycle is the monthly period (approximately 30 days) during which your credit card transactions are recorded. At the end of each billing cycle, a statement is generated showing all transactions and the total amount due.

### Q: What is the minimum amount due (MAD) on a credit card?
**A:** The minimum amount due is the smallest payment you must make by the payment due date to avoid late payment charges. At NovaBank, the MAD is 5% of the total outstanding or INR 200, whichever is higher. However, paying only the MAD results in finance charges on the remaining balance.

### Q: What are finance charges on a credit card?
**A:** Finance charges are the interest charged on outstanding credit card balances carried beyond the payment due date. NovaBank's finance charges range from 36.00% p.a. (Platinum, 3.00% per month) to 42.00% p.a. (Classic, 3.50% per month). No finance charges apply if the full outstanding is paid by the due date.
